Holiday Convoy Forecast

Plan before you spend

This tool helps you estimate Convoy Tokens, compare reward costs, and save different what-if Scenarios before committing valuable resources in game.

Quick Start

  1. Choose an existing example Scenario or create a new one.
  2. Add the resources and Daily Mission Sets you expect to acquire/complete.
  3. Select the rewards you want under Planned Acquisitions.
  4. Compare your Token Budget and Expected Cost, then adjust to hit your goals.
